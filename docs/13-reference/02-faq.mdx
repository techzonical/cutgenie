# Frequently asked questions

**Is Cutgenie free?**
Yes. It is free and open source, under the GPL-3.0 licence.

**Which Macs does it run on?**
See [What you need](../01-getting-started/02-requirements.mdx).

**Does Cutgenie change my video files?**
No. Edits live only in the project file. Your recordings are never changed, moved or deleted, even by Claude.

**Where are my projects saved?**
Wherever you chose when you made them. **All projects…** (⇧⌘O) lists them, and **Reveal in Finder** shows the file.

**Can I open Final Cut Pro or Premiere Pro projects?**
Final Cut edits can come in as FCPXML (see [Import a Final Cut Pro edit](../04-importing/05-fcpxml-import.mdx)).
Premiere Pro projects can't be opened.

**I made a mistake and saved. Can I go back?**
Yes. **Versions…** keeps your last 10 saves. See [Versions](../03-projects/06-versions.mdx).

**Do I need Claude to use Cutgenie?**
No. Everything works by hand. Claude is an optional helper; see [Editing with Claude](../12-editing-with-claude/01-how-it-works.mdx).

**Can someone on the internet control my Cutgenie?**
No. Cutgenie only accepts commands from your own Mac (`127.0.0.1`).

**What should I export for YouTube?**
The **YouTube** preset. See [Which preset to use](../10-exporting/02-presets.mdx).

**Why is my exported file so big?**
**Master** (ProRes) files are very large on purpose. For sharing, use **YouTube** or **Smart size**.
