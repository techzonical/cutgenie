# Sync a separate microphone

Many people record the voice on a separate microphone or recorder because it sounds better than the camera.
Cutgenie can line the two up for you by comparing their sound. This is called **syncing by sound**.

1. Put the camera clip on the timeline.
2. Drag the microphone recording onto a sound track under it, roughly in the right place.
3. Select both clips (click one, then **⌘-click** the other).
4. Right-click and choose **Sync with audio** (or press **⌥⌘G**).

Cutgenie slides the microphone clip so both match. Then you can turn the camera's own sound down or off in the
camera clip's **Volume**.

**Tip:** clap once in front of the camera at the start of each recording. It gives a sharp sound that's easy to match.
