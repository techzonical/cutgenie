# Organise your media

Long videos mean lots of clips. These help you find the good ones:

- **Favourite**: select a clip and press **F** (or right-click ▸ **Favourite**). The *Favourites* filter shows only
  these.
- **Reject**: press **⌫** on a clip you won't use. It's hidden from the normal view. Your file is not deleted.
- **Unused**: this filter shows clips not yet in the current project.
- **Collections**: click **+ ▸ New collection…** to make a group, like "B-roll" or "Interviews". Drag clips into it.
  Deleting a collection never deletes media.
- **Keywords and note**: right-click a clip ▸ **Keywords and note…**. Search finds these words later.
- **Rename…**, **Show in Finder** and **Remove from library** are also in the right-click menu.
  *Remove from library* only removes it from the list; the file stays on your Mac.
