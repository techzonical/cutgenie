# Coming from Final Cut Pro or Premiere Pro

Cutgenie borrows the ideas that work best in **Final Cut Pro** (Apple) and **Premiere Pro** (Adobe), and puts them in
one simple editor. New to editing? You can skip this page.

## Ideas from Final Cut Pro

| In Cutgenie | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| **Magnetic main track**: clips close up when you delete or move one | No accidental gaps |
| **Skimming**: point at a clip to preview it, no clicking | Find the right moment fast |
| **One-key tools**: A Select, B Blade, T Trim, P Pen, Z Zoom, H Hand | Your Final Cut habits still work |
| **W** insert, **E** append, **⇧⌘V** paste attributes, **⌥-drag** to copy | Same keys as Final Cut |
| Drag the **volume line** on a clip, **⌥-click** for a keyframe | Quick sound changes on the timeline |
| **Builds** and **behaviours** on text (slide in, typewriter, drift, wiggle) | Motion-style animation without a second app |
| Saved **titles** you reuse in any project | Your lower thirds stay consistent |
| **FCPXML import** | Bring in an edit made in Final Cut |

## Ideas from Premiere Pro

| In Cutgenie | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| **Numbered tracks** (V1, V2, A1, A2) that you can add freely | Easy to see what is on top of what |
| **Adjustment layers** | Change the look of many clips at once |
| **Basic colour** (Exposure, Contrast, Temperature, Tint) plus **colour wheels** and **LUTs** | A familiar Lumetri-style workflow |
| **Effects per sound track** (gate, EQ, compressor, limiter) | Set up a voice once for the whole track |
| **Measure and match loudness** to −14 LUFS | YouTube-ready volume in one click |
| **Export in the background** while you keep editing | No waiting |
| **J K L**, **I** / **O** for in and out points | Same keys as Premiere (and Final Cut) |

## Only in Cutgenie

- **Edit with an AI assistant.** Ask Claude to make the cuts, then check and adjust them by hand.
- **Your work is kept safe for you**: autosave every minute, the last 10 versions of every project, and crash recovery.
- **Blur areas** to hide a face or private text, built in.
- **Free and open source.**

## What is different

- Cutgenie is a young app. It does not have everything the big editors have, such as multicam editing or a separate
  effects app.
- Final Cut Pro *libraries* and Premiere Pro *project files* can't be opened. Final Cut edits can come in as FCPXML.
