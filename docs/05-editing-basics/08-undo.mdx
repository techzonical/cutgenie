# Undo and redo

Made a mistake? Press **⌘Z** to undo it. Press again to undo the change before that.

Changed your mind? **⇧⌘Z** redoes what you undid.

The ↶ and ↷ buttons at the bottom of the tool column do the same.

You can undo almost everything, including edits made by Claude. For bigger steps back, see
[Go back to an earlier version](../03-projects/06-versions.mdx).
