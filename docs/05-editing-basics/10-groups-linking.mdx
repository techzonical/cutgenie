# Groups and linked sound

**Linked sound**: a camera clip's picture and sound are **linked**, so they move together. To move the sound on its
own (for example to start it a bit early), select the clip and press **⌘L** to unlink. Press **⌘L** again with both
selected to link them back. **⌃⇧S** puts the sound on its own clip.

Hold **⌥** and click to pick just one part of a linked pair.

**Groups**: select several clips (a video, a title and a sound effect, say) and press **⌘G**. They now move together
as one. **⇧⌘G** ungroups. Right-click ▸ **Rename group…** to give the group a name.
