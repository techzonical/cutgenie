# Add clips to the timeline

Select a clip in the media panel, then:

| Key | Name | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **E** | Append | Adds the clip at the **end** of your edit. |
| **W** | Insert | Puts it **at the playhead**. Clips after it move right to make room. |
| **Q** | Connect | Places it **above** the main track at the playhead, like a cutaway (B-roll). |

You can also drag a clip from the media panel to any spot on the timeline.

**Use only part of a clip**: skim the clip in the media panel, press **I** where the good part starts and **O**
where it ends. Then **E**, **W** or **Q** only adds that part.
