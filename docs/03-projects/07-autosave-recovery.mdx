# Autosave and crash recovery

You can't lose much work in Cutgenie:

- **Autosave**: every minute, Cutgenie writes your unsaved changes to a safe place on your Mac.
- **Crash recovery**: if Cutgenie or your Mac closes unexpectedly, open the project again. Cutgenie offers to
  **restore the unsaved work**. Click to restore it, or discard it to keep the last saved version.
- **Safe saving**: a save is written in full before it replaces the old file, so a power cut can't leave a
  half-written project.

It's still good to press **⌘S** often. *Saved just now* above the viewer tells you you're up to date.
