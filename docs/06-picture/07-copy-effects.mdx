# Copy effects to other clips

**Video clips.** Did one clip get the right position, zoom, crop or colour? Copy it to others.

1. Select the clip that looks right and press **⌘C**.
2. Select the other clips (⌘-click to pick several).
3. Press **⇧⌘V** to paste everything. Or right-click a clip and choose **Paste attributes from …**, then pick only
   one part: **Position, scale, rotation, opacity**, **Zoom**, **Crop**, **Drop shadow** or **Colour**.

**Text and shape layers.** Select the layer and open the **Effects** menu at the top of the inspector:
**Copy effects**, then select other layers and choose **Paste effects**. The same menu has **Reset effects**,
**Save as preset…** (keeps the settings for any project) and **Apply preset**.

For colour only, you can also use **Copy grade** in the Color tab: see [Save and reuse a look](../07-colour/05-looks.mdx).
