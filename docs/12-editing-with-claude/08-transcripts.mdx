# Transcripts: Claude listens to your video

A **transcript** is everything you say in a video, written down, with the time of every word. With a transcript, Claude
can do things like *"add a lower third when I say my name"* without you finding any times yourself.

Everything happens **on your Mac**. Your video is not uploaded, and it is never changed.

## What you need (once)

Two free things, both on your Mac:

1. **Whisper**, the program that turns speech into text.
2. **A speech model**, a file Whisper uses to recognise words. There are three sizes; see the next section.

You don't have to set these up before you start. The first time you ask for something that needs a transcript, Claude
tells you what's missing and **asks before installing or downloading anything**.

**Installing Whisper**

- **In Claude Code:** say yes when Claude asks, and it installs Whisper for you.
- **In the Claude app:** the app can't install programs, so you do this one step yourself. Open **Terminal** (press
  **⌘ Space**, type *Terminal*, press Return), type this and press Return:

  ```bash
  brew install whisper-cpp
  ```

  This needs [Homebrew](https://brew.sh). If Terminal says `brew: command not found`, install Homebrew first by
  following the one line on that page.

**Downloading a speech model:** Claude does this for you in both apps, after you pick a model and say yes.

## Which speech model to pick

Think of the models as small, medium and big brains. A bigger one understands more but is a bigger download.

| Model | Download | Pick it if… |
|---|---|---|
| **base** | 150 MB | you speak **only English**, clearly. Fastest. |
| **small** | 490 MB | you speak English with an accent or some background noise. |
| **large** | 1.6 GB | you speak **Hindi, Hinglish (mixed Hindi and English), or any language other than English**, or the smaller ones made mistakes. The best. |

**Not sure? Pick large.** It works for everything, and it is still quick: about 20 seconds for 5 minutes of talking on a
recent Mac. The only cost is the bigger download, once.

> **Speaking Hindi or Hinglish?** Pick **large**. The smaller models often give you an English translation instead of
> your own words, write Hindi in the wrong script (for example Urdu), or repeat the same few words over and over.

You can have more than one model. Claude always uses the biggest one you have. To get a different one later, ask:
*Download the large speech model.*

## How to ask

1. Open your project in Cutgenie.
2. Ask Claude in words. **Don't attach or upload your video to the chat** (see below). For example:
   - *Write down what I say in my first clip.*
   - *Transcribe my first clip in Hindi.*
   - *Add a lower third when I say my name.* Claude makes the transcript first, then places it.

Long videos are transcribed 5 minutes at a time, so a 20-minute video takes a few small steps. Claude does that by
itself.

**For Hinglish**, tell Claude how you want it written: Hindi in Devanagari (*आज हम review करेंगे*) or everything in
English letters (*aaj hum review karenge*).

## The first time, step by step

This is what a first conversation looks like in the Claude app:

1. You: *Add a lower third when I say my name.*
2. Claude: Whisper isn't installed yet. Claude tells you the Terminal command above. Run it, then say *done*.
3. Claude: no speech model yet. Claude shows you the three choices and asks which one.
4. You: *Large, please.*
5. Claude downloads it (a few minutes, once), writes the transcript, finds your name and adds the lower third.
6. Claude tells you which words it used and when, so you can check by watching.

Next time, steps 2 to 4 are skipped.

## When the words come out wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Your Hindi came back in **English** | A small model translated it | Ask: *Download the large speech model and try again in Hindi.* |
| Hindi in the **wrong script** (like Urdu) | A small model guessed wrong | Same: use **large**, and say *in Hindi*. |
| The **same words repeated** over and over | The model got stuck | Use **large**. If it still happens, ask Claude to try a shorter part. |
| Claude says *"no speech-to-text model"* or *"package installs are blocked"* | You attached the video, so Claude worked on a copy online instead of using Cutgenie | Start a new chat **without attaching anything** and ask again. |
| Claude says Whisper isn't installed | Whisper isn't on this Mac yet | Run `brew install whisper-cpp` in Terminal (see above). |
| Some English words come out a bit odd in Hindi text | Normal for mixed speech | Ask Claude to fix them, or edit the title text yourself. |

## Where the files are, and freeing space

The speech models are kept in your Library folder, in `Application Support/Cutgenie/whisper`. To free space, open
**Finder**, choose **Go ▸ Go to Folder…**, paste `~/Library/Application Support/Cutgenie/whisper` and delete a model
file you no longer want. Claude asks before downloading it again if it's ever needed.
