# Tips for asking

- **Use times.** *At 1:12* or *from 0:30 to 0:45* is clearer than *near the middle*.
- **Name clips the way Cutgenie shows them**, for example *clip 3* or *the clip called intro.mp4*.
- **One job at a time** for big changes. Check it, then ask for the next.
- **Say how much.** *A little brighter*, *zoom in 10 %*, *pauses longer than 1 second*.
- **Ask what it did.** *What did you change?* gets a plain list.
- **Go back any time.** *Undo that* or *go back to the version from this morning*.

If Claude says it can't reach Cutgenie, make sure Cutgenie is open, then ask again.
