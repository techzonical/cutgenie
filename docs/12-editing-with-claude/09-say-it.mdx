# Example: place things by what you say

You don't need to find timestamps. Claude can listen to your video and put things where you say them.

1. Open your project in Cutgenie.
2. Ask Claude, for example:
   - *Add a lower third with my name when I say my name.*
   - *Zoom in a little when I say "price".*
   - *Put a title "Battery life" up when I start talking about the battery.*
   - *Cut out the part where I say "let me try that again".*

Claude:

- writes down everything you say in the clip, with the time of every word (this is called a **transcript**),
- finds the words you asked about,
- puts the title, lower third, zoom or cut there, starting a moment early so it doesn't feel late,
- tells you which words it used and when (*"You say your name at 0:12, so the lower third runs 0:12 to 0:17"*).

3. Press **Space** to watch. If something is in the wrong place, press **⌘Z**, or ask Claude to move it:
   *Start the lower third half a second later.*

## How it listens

Claude first writes down what you say (a **transcript**), using **Whisper** on your Mac. Your video is never uploaded
and never changed. The first time, Claude asks before installing Whisper and a speech model.

**If you speak Hindi or Hinglish, pick the large speech model.** Everything about this, step by step, is on
[Transcripts: Claude listens to your video](08-transcripts.mdx).
